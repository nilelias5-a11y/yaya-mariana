## Executive Summary

Yaya Mariana operates at the intersection of two well-documented Spanish market dynamics: (1) a strawberry sector overwhelmingly concentrated in Huelva (~97 % of the ~350,000 t national production in 2023; ~210,345 t in the 2024/25 campaign with a 5 % YoY drop, and a further ~50 % cut for 2025/26 after storms) ([VerticalFarmDaily, 2024](https://www.verticalfarmdaily.com/article/9556552/huelva-already-accounts-for-97-of-spain-s-strawberry-production/); [Blueberries Consulting, 2025](https://blueberriesconsulting.com/en/concluye-la-campana-24-25-de-frutos-rojos-en-huelva-con-descenso-en-la-produccion-pero-un-repunte-en-las-exportaciones/); [ItalianBerry, 2025](https://italianberry.it/en/news/huelva-spain-strawberries-production-halved-storms-season-2025-2026)), and (2) a fast-recovering Spanish food e-commerce channel (5.7 % of food revenue online by 2025, +1.8 % e-commerce growth in Dec 2025, mixed-channel grocery shoppers projected +7.1 pp) ([Statista, 2025](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/food/spain); [ESM Magazine, 2025](https://www.esmmagazine.com/technology/spanish-food-e-commerce-market-set-to-recover-in-2025-290886)). Catalonia is the *second* Spanish strawberry region after Huelva — historically associated with Maresme (Barcelona coast); Tarragona is a smaller niche, which means Yaya Mariana's origin story is genuinely scarce ([El Confidencial Digital, 2024](https://www.elconfidencialdigital.com/articulo/consumo/fresas-viaje-mejores-regiones-productoras-espana/20240309000000739247.html)). Premium drivers are *converging* in Yaya Mariana's favour: 62 % of consumers rank pesticides/ultra-processing above price as a concern ([PwC Voice of the Consumer, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html)); 61 % actively seek nacional + temporada ([AECOC via FoodRetail, 2025](https://www.foodretail.es/food/consumo-fruta-verdura-hortalizas-espana_0_1542145802.html)); national-fresh-product preference reaches 9-in-10 consumers ([Autonomos y Emprendedor, 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/nueve-cada-diez-consumidores-espanoles-prefieren-comprar-negocios-producto-fresco-nacional/20250801124531044554.html)). The existing `clasico` build's positioning ("del campo a tu mesa", sin pesticidas, named varieties, abuela narrative, Tarragona origin) is therefore *empirically* aligned with the dominant 2025-2026 premium-DTC playbook — the audit phases (4.5, 5) should surgically *amplify* these signals, not replace them.

## Industry Snapshot

**Market size and structure**
- Spain produces ~400,000 t of strawberries per year — the largest producer in Europe, ~50 % of EU output ([FoodsWinesFromSpain, 2025](https://www.foodswinesfromspain.com/en/food/articles/2025/may/the-red-allure-of-spanish-strawberry)).
- Huelva = ~97 % of Spanish production (350,000 t base in 2023); 2024/25 campaign closed at 210,345 t (-5 % YoY) ([Blueberries Consulting, 2025](https://blueberriesconsulting.com/en/concluye-la-campana-24-25-de-frutos-rojos-en-huelva-con-descenso-en-la-produccion-pero-un-repunte-en-las-exportaciones/)); 2025/26 storm damage projected ~50 % production cut ([ItalianBerry, 2025](https://italianberry.it/en/news/huelva-spain-strawberries-production-halved-storms-season-2025-2026)).
- Catalonia is recognized as the **second** strawberry region nationally, with Maresme (coastal Barcelona) historically the most appreciated micro-origin; Tarragona/Camp de Tarragona is a smaller, less-saturated micro-zone ([El Confidencial Digital, 2024](https://www.elconfidencialdigital.com/articulo/consumo/fresas-viaje-mejores-regiones-productoras-espana/20240309000000739247.html); [Ir Oui Come, 2024](https://www.irouicome.com/blog/es/pais-catalan/especialidades-y-saber-hacer/la-region-catalana-tirra-de-frutas-tradicion-y-playas/fresa-el-comienzo-de-la-primavera/)).
- Dominant Huelva varieties for 2025/26: **RedSayra** (Planasa) leading at 13.5 % share, alongside Marisma and the premium-priced **Rossetta® / NSG 465** for the high-end segment ([Portalfrutícola, 2026](https://www.portalfruticola.com/noticias/2026/03/16/redsayra-plansa-fresa/); [HortiDaily, 2025](https://www.hortidaily.es/article/9818002/cuales-son-las-variedades-de-fresa-mas-cultivadas-este-ano-en-espana/); [Agronegocios, 2025](https://www.agronegocios.es/agronegocios/nsg-consolida-su-posicion-en-variedades-de-fresas-con-58-millones-de-plantas-en-huelva-para-la-campana-2025/)). Yaya Mariana's varieties (Mágnum, Dream, 1525) are *outside* the Huelva mainstream — defensible as "no comodificadas".

**Regulatory / quality-seal context**
- Catalonia holds 12 DOP + 10 IGP food designations recognized by the EU; relevant to Tarragona is the **IGP Calçot de Valls** (Alt Camp, Baix Camp, Tarragonès, Baix Penedès) ([Federació Catalana DOP-IGP](https://www.federaciodopigp.cat/en); [MAPA Calçot IGP](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/alimentacion/temas/calidad-diferenciada/dop-igp/detalle/hortalizas/igp_calcotvalls)). **No DO/IGP currently covers Catalan strawberries** — meaning Yaya Mariana cannot lean on an existing seal, but the *terroir vocabulary* of nearby IGPs (Tarragonès, Camp de Tarragona) is publicly understood and adjacent.

**Season + price elasticity**
- Spanish strawberry season runs Feb–Jun, peak quality Mar–May; out-of-season volume drops sharply and prices invert (low supply = high price) ([Matundy](https://matundy.es/temporada-de-fresas-y-fresones-en-espana/); [ComeFruta](https://comefruta.es/temporada-de-fresas-y-fresones)).
- Negative correlation between volume and supermarket price is well-documented; premium DTC sits in the high-price/low-volume quadrant and benefits from the off-peak gap ([Weekontrol](https://weekontrol.com/noticias/cuando-es-temporada-de-fresas-y-por-que-es-mejor-comprarlas-en-su-epoca/)).

## Digital Landscape

**Channel growth**
- Food = 5.7 % of online retail revenue in Spain in 2025; food/grocery e-commerce recovering after two inflationary years ([Statista, 2025](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/food/spain); [ESM Magazine, 2025](https://www.esmmagazine.com/technology/spanish-food-e-commerce-market-set-to-recover-in-2025-290886); [Revista Mercados](https://revistamercados.com/futuro-prometedor-para-las-ventas-de-frutas-y-hortalizas-online/)).
- Online fresh-produce market in Spain is fragmented (≤100–150 webshops per country) — fresh fruit/veg now purchased online more frequently than other fresh categories ([FoodRetail, 2024](https://www.foodretail.es/especiales/sector-primario/ecommerce-de-frescos-en-espana-esta-el-mercado-preparado-para-una-fruteria-y-verduleria-cien-por-cien-online.html)).
- Spain grocery+food market reached USD 197.6 B in 2024, projected USD 259.5 B by 2033 (CAGR 3.1 %) ([IMARC, 2025](https://www.imarcgroup.com/spain-grocery-food-market)).

**Key players and pricing**
- **CrowdFarming** (Madrid, founded 2017, ex-Naranjas del Carmen): 200+ farmers, 350,000 EU consumers, adoption + harvest model; pricing observed €3.53–€45.59/kg across categories; main markets ES/DE/FR/AT/NL ([CrowdFarming](https://www.crowdfarming.com/); [Green Product Award](https://www.gp-award.com/en/products/crowdfarming); [SlimPay](https://www.slimpay.com/blog/naranjas-del-carmen-business-model-sustainability-efficiency/)).
- **Naranjas del Carmen**: originator of "crowdfarming" model (2015), pesticide-free, financial gain 5× wholesale via DTC ([EIB](https://www.eib.org/en/stories/food-sustainable-agriculture-direct-selling); [Resilience, 2020](https://www.resilience.org/stories/2020-02-27/from-root-to-fruit-zero-waste-crowdfarming-in-valencia/)).
- **Frutas Charito** (Madrid, 60+ years): same-day delivery, 100 % electric fleet — exemplifies premium-fresh DTC with local brand heritage ([Frutas Charito](https://frutascharito.es/en/)).
- Premium fruit-basket gifting platforms (sprimfruit, Take Fruit, Catas Gourmet, Frutería Online Madrid) explicitly pair *Fresones del Maresme* with Cava in curated gift sets — confirming a paid premium for Catalan-origin strawberries in the gifting channel ([Catas Gourmet, 2025](https://www.catasgourmet.com/cesta-de-frutas/); [Frutería Online Madrid](https://fruteriaonlinemadrid.es/cestas-de-fruta/cesta-de-fruta-premium/)).

**Dominant design patterns observed in premium produce DTC (2025-2026)**
- **Hero with farm video / harvest day storytelling**, founder/family profile, behind-the-scenes is now table-stakes for emotional differentiation ([Penn State Extension, 2025](https://extension.psu.edu/top-fresh-food-marketing-trends-for-2025); [Farmonaut, 2025](https://farmonaut.com/blogs/strategic-agribusiness-marketing-5-powerful-trends-for-2025)).
- **Hyper-local + regenerative sourcing** narrative; consumer trust earned by traceability tech, real-time tracking, transparency on practices ([Farmonaut, 2025](https://farmonaut.com/blogs/farming-pr-agency-7-marketing-trends-for-2025); [Number Analytics, 2025](https://www.numberanalytics.com/blog/future-of-farm-to-table-trends-opportunities)).
- **Seasonal calendar / pre-order windows** tied to harvest urgency — borrows urgency from the real calendar instead of fabricating scarcity ([ConvertCart](https://www.convertcart.com/blog/hero-image-examples-ecommerce); [Baymard, 2024](https://baymard.com/blog/grocery-site-ux-launch)).
- **Recipe hubs + Trustpilot/review integration** to drive engagement and trust ([Limely, 2025](https://www.limely.co.uk/blog/top-14-grocery-ecommerce-websites-in-2025)).
- **High-resolution close-ups + evocative copy** ("Taste freshness in every bite" tier) — premium-but-approachable visual rhythm ([ConvertCart](https://www.convertcart.com/blog/hero-image-examples-ecommerce)).

**Table-stakes features (non-negotiable for fresh DTC in Spain 2025-2026)**
- Cold-chain / shipping clarity (24–48 h delivery promise, EPS or refrigerated packaging explained) ([Revista Logistec, 2026](https://www.revistalogistec.com/index.php/2026/03/04/6-tendencias-que-marcaran-la-logistica-de-transporte-refrigerado-en-2026/); [Knauf Industries](https://knauf-industries.es/transporte-alimentos-congelados/)).
- Origin traceability (farm name, plot, harvest date or batch) — leverages 61 % "nacional + temporada" preference ([AECOC via FoodRetail, 2025](https://www.foodretail.es/food/consumo-fruta-verdura-hortalizas-espana_0_1542145802.html)).
- Allergen / cultivation method disclosures (no-pesticide claims must be substantiated, since 62 % of consumers rank pesticides above price as concern — but unsubstantiated claims trigger backlash) ([PwC Voice of the Consumer, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html); [Zerya, 2025](https://zerya.org/2025/02/03/consumidores-europeos-exigen-alimentos-sin-pesticidas-zerya-genera-confianza/)).
- Stripe Checkout / one-page checkout — Yaya Mariana's existing single-page + Stripe stack is *current best practice* for this segment.
- Schema.org Product + LocalBusiness / Organization structured data (research did not surface a single canonical adoption study, but Baymard's grocery UX research and general e-commerce SEO consensus treats it as table-stakes for product visibility ([Baymard, 2024](https://baymard.com/blog/grocery-site-ux-launch))).

## Target Market

**Who buys premium Spanish strawberries DTC**
- Spanish urban households (Madrid, Barcelona, regional capitals) with above-average disposable income; PwC's 2025 Voice of the Consumer finds 62 % rank pesticides/ultra-processing above price, value, or sustainability as their top food concern ([PwC, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html)).
- 9-in-10 Spanish consumers prefer to buy from businesses selling national fresh produce; 61 % actively seek nacional + en-temporada ([Autónomos y Emprendedor, 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/nueve-cada-diez-consumidores-espanoles-prefieren-comprar-negocios-producto-fresco-nacional/20250801124531044554.html); [AECOC via FoodRetail, 2025](https://www.foodretail.es/food/consumo-fruta-verdura-hortalizas-espana_0_1542145802.html)).
- 1-in-3 want more pesticide-free / ecological options ([AECOC, 2025](https://www.aecoc.es/articulos/la-sostenibilidad-el-nuevo-driver-del-comprador-de-frutas-y-hortalizas/)).
- Gifting buyers (corporate baskets, regalo saludable) — confirmed premium tier paying for Maresme-origin fresones paired with Cava ([Catas Gourmet, 2025](https://www.catasgourmet.com/cesta-de-frutas/); [Frutería Online Madrid](https://fruteriaonlinemadrid.es/cestas-de-fruta/cesta-de-fruta-premium/); [Take Fruit](https://takefruit.es/regalos-corporativos/cestas-de-fruta-para-regalar/)).

**Purchase drivers (ranked by 2025 data)**
1. **Calidad-precio relationship** = leading "where + what to buy" driver ([AECOC, 2025](https://www.aecoc.es/articulos/la-sostenibilidad-el-nuevo-driver-del-comprador-de-frutas-y-hortalizas/)).
2. **Pesticide avoidance + clean food** = 62 % rank above price ([PwC, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html)).
3. **National origin + seasonality** = 61–90 % depending on phrasing ([AECOC, 2025](https://www.foodretail.es/food/consumo-fruta-verdura-hortalizas-espana_0_1542145802.html); [Autónomos y Emprendedor, 2025](https://www.autonomosyemprendedor.es/articulo/autonomos/nueve-cada-diez-consumidores-espanoles-prefieren-comprar-negocios-producto-fresco-nacional/20250801124531044554.html)).
4. **Brand recognition / known marca** for fresh produce — actively builds confianza ([Revista Mercados](https://revistamercados.com/el-consumidor-en-el-centro-de-las-frutas-y-hortalizas/)).
5. **Traceability + supply-chain transparency** — increasingly attractive ([Revista Mercados](https://revistamercados.com/el-consumidor-en-el-centro-de-las-frutas-y-hortalizas/); [Farmonaut, 2025](https://farmonaut.com/blogs/farming-pr-agency-7-marketing-trends-for-2025)).

**Seasonality & demand pattern**
- Demand peaks Mar–May (in-season); valle Jul–Jan. Strawberries are NOT a year-round DTC product unless company invests in greenhouse / extended-season varieties.
- The 2025/26 Huelva storm-cut creates a **scarcity window** (less supply, higher market prices) where Catalan small-producer narratives carry extra weight ([ItalianBerry, 2025](https://italianberry.it/en/news/huelva-spain-strawberries-production-halved-storms-season-2025-2026)).

## Digital SWOT — Yaya Mariana (clasico build)

**Strengths** (internal, current in `clasico`)
- "Del campo a tu mesa" + abuela storytelling + sin pesticidas = textbook 2025-2026 premium-DTC playbook ([Penn State Extension, 2025](https://extension.psu.edu/top-fresh-food-marketing-trends-for-2025); [Farmonaut, 2025](https://farmonaut.com/blogs/strategic-agribusiness-marketing-5-powerful-trends-for-2025)).

- Tarragona origin = inside the Camp de Tarragona / IGP Calçot zone, adjacent to recognized Catalan terroir vocabulary ([MAPA Calçot IGP](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/alimentacion/temas/calidad-diferenciada/dop-igp/detalle/hortalizas/igp_calcotvalls)).

**Weaknesses** (internal, surgical-improvement candidates)
- Unsubstantiated "sin pesticidas" claims are a 2025 backlash risk if not paired with concrete proof / certificación / Zerya-style verification ([Zerya, 2025](https://zerya.org/2025/02/03/consumidores-europeos-exigen-alimentos-sin-pesticidas-zerya-genera-confianza/)) — content-strategist + trust signals (manual) territory.
- No public DO/IGP exists for Catalan strawberries → cannot lean on a seal; must build own credibility (founder bio, harvest date, plot photos) — trust-signals owner.
- Single-page sites can underperform on long-tail SEO for "fresas Tarragona", "fresas sin pesticidas online", "comprar fresas premium" if H1/H2/schema not surgically tuned — seo-genius territory.
- Cold-chain / shipping-clarity messaging is a known DTC fresh-produce conversion lever ([Knauf](https://knauf-industries.es/transporte-alimentos-congelados/); [Revista Logistec, 2026](https://www.revistalogistec.com/index.php/2026/03/04/6-tendencias-que-marcaran-la-logistica-de-transporte-refrigerado-en-2026/)) — conversion-funnel-optimizer territory.
- Strawberries are seasonal; off-season the site needs a story (waitlist, próxima cosecha, plant-now-receive-in-May) — content-strategist + conversion-funnel-optimizer.

**Opportunities** (external, can capture without redesign)
- The Huelva 2025/26 production crash (~50 % cut) creates a public-relations window where small Catalan growers gain media oxygen ([ItalianBerry, 2025](https://italianberry.it/en/news/huelva-spain-strawberries-production-halved-storms-season-2025-2026)).
- Premium gifting (corporate, regalo saludable) confirmed paying for Maresme-origin fresones — same logic extends to Tarragona ([Catas Gourmet, 2025](https://www.catasgourmet.com/cesta-de-frutas/); [Take Fruit](https://takefruit.es/regalos-corporativos/cestas-de-fruta-para-regalar/)).

**Threats** (external, must defend against)
- **Carrefour Bio** + **Alcampo / El Corte Inglés / Eroski** premium organic private label = the dominant alternative for the "pesticide-conscious + convenience" segment ([Carrefour Group](https://www.carrefour.com/en/objectif-rse-bio-accessible); [ESM Magazine](https://www.esmmagazine.com/retail/top-10-supermarket-retail-chains-in-spain-229277); [InSpain News](https://inspain.news/which-supermarket-in-spain-has-the-most-organic-products/)). - Cold-chain / last-mile is the highest-failure-rate cost line in fresh DTC (a few degrees of variance ruins a shipment) ([Revista Logistec, 2025](https://www.revistalogistec.com/index.php/2025/11/27/ecommerce-de-alimentos-esta-redefiniendo-la-logistica-refrigerada/)). One bad delivery cycle = reviews damage.
- Climate volatility (Huelva storms 2025/26) eventually reaches Catalonia too — single-region producers are more exposed, not less ([ItalianBerry, 2025](https://italianberry.it/en/news/huelva-spain-strawberries-production-halved-storms-season-2025-2026); [IntechOpen, 2024](https://www.intechopen.com/chapters/1211517)).

## Strategic Implications (AUDIT framing — what to lever, what to keep)

Each implication is framed as either **VALIDATES** (keep current `clasico` choice) or **SURGICAL LEVER** (improve in a specific audit/optimization phase, with owning agent).

1. **VALIDATES — abuela / sin-pesticidas / del-campo-a-tu-mesa narrative.** This is *empirically the dominant 2025-2026 playbook* for premium produce DTC ([Penn State Extension, 2025](https://extension.psu.edu/top-fresh-food-marketing-trends-for-2025); [Farmonaut, 2025](https://farmonaut.com/blogs/strategic-agribusiness-marketing-5-powerful-trends-for-2025); [PwC, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html)). Phases 4.5/5 should keep the narrative architecture intact and *amplify* it with concrete proof (founder portrait quality, harvest-day video, plot coordinates). **Owner: content-strategist.**

2. **VALIDATES — named varieties (Mágnum, Dream, 1525).** Outside the commodity Huelva mainstream (RedSayra/Marisma); reinforces ownable scarcity. Keep, do not commodify by hiding behind generic "fresa". **Owner: content-strategist + seo-genius (long-tail variety queries).**

3. **VALIDATES — Tarragona/Catalunya origin.** Adjacent to recognized IGP Camp de Tarragona terroir vocabulary; second strawberry region in Spain after Huelva; Maresme already commands gifting premium → Tarragona is a defensible micro-origin claim ([MAPA Calçot IGP](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/alimentacion/temas/calidad-diferenciada/dop-igp/detalle/hortalizas/igp_calcotvalls); [El Confidencial Digital, 2024](https://www.elconfidencialdigital.com/articulo/consumo/fresas-viaje-mejores-regiones-productoras-espana/20240309000000739247.html)). Audit must surface this MORE prominently (locale schema, "fresas de Tarragona" H1 variants). **Owner: seo-genius + content-strategist.**

4. **SURGICAL LEVER — substantiate the "sin pesticidas" claim with verifiable trust signals.** 62 % rank pesticides above price ([PwC, 2025](https://www.pwc.es/es/retail-consumo/voice-of-the-consumer-survey-2025.html)) — but Zerya 2025 explicitly warns that unsubstantiated claims trigger consumer rejection ([Zerya, 2025](https://zerya.org/2025/02/03/consumidores-europeos-exigen-alimentos-sin-pesticidas-zerya-genera-confianza/)). Add: harvest method explainer section, batch / harvest-date display, founder name + face, plot photo with date stamp. **Owner: trust signals (manual section, per project brief that replaces trust-builder) + content-strategist.**

5. **SURGICAL LEVER — schema.org Product + LocalBusiness + harvest date + price.** Spanish premium DTC competitors (CrowdFarming, Frutas Charito) win long-tail visibility through structured data. The single-page Stripe architecture stays; only the head/markup grows. **Owner: seo-genius. Phase 4.5.**

6. **SURGICAL LEVER — cold-chain / shipping-clarity proof above the fold or near CTA.** A few degrees of variance ruins a shipment; competitors that explain their packaging convert better ([Knauf](https://knauf-industries.es/transporte-alimentos-congelados/); [Revista Logistec, 2026](https://www.revistalogistec.com/index.php/2026/03/04/6-tendencias-que-marcaran-la-logistica-de-transporte-refrigerado-en-2026/)). Add a "Cómo llega tu fresa" micro-section explaining 24-48 h península, refrigerated parcel, insulation. **Owner: conversion-funnel-optimizer.**

7. **SURGICAL LEVER — seasonal calendar + off-season story.** Strawberry season is Feb-Jun ([Matundy](https://matundy.es/temporada-de-fresas-y-fresones-en-espana/)). Yaya Mariana's site must not feel "broken" Jul-Jan: add a próxima cosecha countdown / waitlist signup / "te avisamos cuando vuelvan". **Owner: conversion-funnel-optimizer + content-strategist.**

8. **SURGICAL LEVER — defend the Catalan-language path as a moat.** CA-native UX is rare in DTC produce — keep the locale routing tight (CA hreflang, CA-first metadata, CA testimonials if any exist) to defend the local-loyalty segment that supermarket Bio cannot match. **Owner: seo-genius (hreflang/canonical audit).**

9. **SURGICAL LEVER — frame "Huelva storms 2025/26" indirectly.** Don't attack Huelva by name (rural-Spain sympathy norm), but the *scarcity context* validates premium pricing right now. A subtle "este año cada fresa cuenta más" beat in copy is defensible. **Owner: content-strategist.**

10. **VALIDATES — Stripe Checkout single-page architecture.** Aligned with grocery UX consensus on reducing friction ([Baymard, 2024](https://baymard.com/blog/grocery-site-ux-launch)). Do not propose multi-step funnel.
