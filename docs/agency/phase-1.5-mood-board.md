**Prepared by:** mood-board-creator

## Why We Did This First

Before refining a single section of the existing site, we studied the best websites in the artisanal-food and heritage-brand space. We re-documented the 5 references the competitor analyst already validated, then added 5 more that fit a *tribute-tone* food brand specifically. From all 10 we synthesised **three visual directions**.

This site is unusual: it sells 500-g punnets of strawberries, and it is also a quiet memorial to a real grandmother. The mood board's job is to make sure the visual language carries **both** truths at once — and to make sure neither one depends on a decision that has not been made yet.

There are no wrong answers among the three directions. The one Nil and the family choose will guide every visual decision in Phase 4.

---

## A Note on How This Mood Board Is Built — the Dual-Path Hero

The single most important constraint of this phase (Risk 1 in the Phase-1 brief): **the family may decline to provide any photography of Mariana.** If the mood board only worked with a photograph, the family conversation could reset the entire design.

So this mood board is built so it **cannot** be reset. Every one of the three directions below specifies its Hero **twice**, in parallel:

- **Path P — Photograph-led:** how the Hero works WITH archival family photography (or, failing that, next-generation family hands).
- **Path T — Typography-only:** how the *same direction's* Hero works with **no photograph at all** — carried by type, space, and the traceability chip.

Both paths are designed up front. Whatever the family decides, the chosen direction survives intact — only the Hero's centre asset swaps. **Path T is not a downgrade.** For a tribute site, a typography-only Hero is arguably the more disciplined, more dignified option. We treat it as a first-class outcome, not a fallback.

### Photography sourcing rule (baked into every direction's Imagery Approach)

1. **First choice:** real archival family photography of Mariana — her hands, her in the field, her kitchen — with surviving-family consent.
2. **Fallback:** next-generation family hands — Nil's mother, or Nil himself — photographed in a single restrained session (hands cradling a Mágnum over warm cream, shallow depth of field, no studio gloss).
3. **Final fallback:** an elegant non-photographic treatment — typography, generous whitespace, or a single product/earthenware still life. **Never stock photography. Never AI-generated imagery of Mariana, ever.**

## Reference 1 — Farm Minerals

**URL:** `farmminerals.com`
**Award:** Awwwards Site of the Day — February 2026
**Niche:** Agriculture / soil & mineral brand
**Year:** 2026

**Visual snapshot:**
- Color palette: deep green `#465626` + cream `#F4EDE6` — strict two-color discipline
- Typography: mixed — a quiet sans for body, a confident display face for headlines
- Layout style: full-bleed sections, generous margins, one focal element per screen
- Motion personality: cinematic-but-slow — GSAP + WebGL scroll-triggered plant-growth motion
- Photography/imagery: minimal; the brand leans on motion and color, not photo volume

**What makes it exceptional:**
- A two-color palette held with total discipline across the entire site — depth comes from scale and space, not from adding colors.
- Scroll *reveals* content (growth metaphor) instead of decorating it — motion has a job.
- Every section has exactly one dominant element. No competing focal points.

**What we can adapt for this client:**
- The two-color discipline is the exact Beat-them plan #5 move — proof that cream + one brand color can carry an entire premium site.
- Scroll-as-reveal mapped onto the existing IntersectionObserver entrances (Beat-them #6) — slow, not springy.

**What does NOT fit:**
- The deep-green base is theirs; Yaya Mariana's base is warm cream, not earthy green.
- The WebGL plant-growth set-piece is a spectacle move — too performative for a memorial register.

# PHASE 3 — Visual Direction Proposals

Three directions. Each is achievable as a **refinement of the existing `clasico` build** — none is a teardown. Each specifies its Hero **twice** (Path P photograph-led and Path T typography-only), per Hard Rule C. Each states its relationship to the cream + strawberry-red + 1-neutral two-color discipline.

---

# Visual Direction A — "La Mesa de la Yaya" (The Grandmother's Table)

**One-line concept:** A warm, domestic still life — the strawberry treated the way she would have placed it on the kitchen table.

**Personality:** Warm · intimate · unhurried

**Inspired by:** Post Familiar Wine · Buly 1803 · Beerenberg Farm

---

## Color Approach
- **Overall palette feeling:** warm and domestic — cream as a tablecloth, strawberry-red as the single piece of fruit on it. Sunlit-kitchen warmth, not gallery coolness.
- **Dominant color role:** light, airy cream base (`#fdf6f5`) dominates; the brand red is the *single* warm punctuation point per screen.
- **Accent use:** sparse and intentional — red appears where the eye should land (the fruit, one CTA, the variety badge) and nowhere else.
- **Two-color discipline:** **lives comfortably inside it.** This direction is the most natural fit for the cream + strawberry-red + 1-neutral system — the warm neutral reads as "wood / linen / kitchen light". No third color needed.

## Typography Approach
- **Heading style:** Playfair Display (kept) — used warmly, at humane sizes, never oversized for drama.
- **Body style:** Inter (kept) — quiet, readable, recedes behind the headings.
- **Weight contrast:** moderate. Headings have presence; nothing shouts. The first-person AboutUs voice sits in a comfortable reading size, not a display size.

## Layout Approach
- **Grid feel:** single-column editorial with calm, generous sections — the page reads like turning pages.
- **Whitespace:** generous breathing room — space signals care, the way an uncrowded table does.
- **Section rhythm:** consistent and gentle. The dark StatsStrip band is *toned down* (smaller numbers, lighter weight) so it no longer jolts after the Hero — addressing the ux-researcher tonal-jolt finding.

## Imagery Approach
- **Photography tone:** warm, natural-light, domestic — soft shadows, the look of a sunlit kitchen mid-morning.
- **Composition:** close-up, intimate scale — hands and fruit, fruit and earthenware. Never panoramic.
- **Treatment:** natural, with a gentle period warmth if archival photography is used. No high-contrast editing, no duotone, no glossy retouch.
- **Sourcing (Hard Rule B):** archival family photography of Mariana → next-gen family hands → elegant non-photographic still life. Never stock, never AI.

### Hero — Path P (photograph-led)
The Hero right-column 380×380 slot holds a **single warm still life**: ideally Mariana's hands (archival) cradling one Mágnum strawberry over warm cream; failing that, next-generation family hands in the same composition. Shallow depth of field, no studio gloss, natural light. The H1 keeps the quiet Playfair quote entry (ux-researcher confirmed this suits the tribute register); the traceability chip and the "Mágnum · Dream · 1525" line sit below it as quiet footnotes. The photograph is the warmth; the type is the calm around it.

### Hero — Path T (typography-only, no photograph)
The **same** Hero, with the 380×380 photo slot replaced by a warm **earthenware-bowl-of-three-strawberries still life rendered as the product photography already needed for the Products section** — or, if even that is declined, by a single typographic block: "Mágnum · Dream · 1525" set large in Playfair Display italic, with the traceability chip beneath. The domestic warmth in Path T comes from the cream texture and the kitchen-warm palette, not from a person. The layout, spacing, and type are unchanged from Path P — only the centre asset differs. The family conversation cannot reset this direction; it only chooses which warm object sits in one slot.

## Motion Approach
- **Animation character:** subtle and refined — slow fades, gentle translate-on-entry (300–500ms ease-out). Nothing springy.
- **Scroll behavior:** simple IntersectionObserver fade reveals; **no scroll-jacking, no pinning** (locked La Nonna resolution). `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected.

## What This Direction Communicates
A visitor feels they have been welcomed into a home, not a shop. The brand reads as warm, careful, and personal — a family that places fruit on a table the way it was always done. The tribute is felt as an undercurrent of warmth rather than stated as a headline. It is the most emotionally *immediate* of the three.

## Best-fit audience
**Núria (gift-buyer)** and **Marc & Helena (tribute-aware curator)** respond most strongly — the domestic warmth makes the product gift-able and makes the tribute land without sentimentality. **Laura** is comfortably served by the calm. Slight risk for **Pau** (cultivar-led) — see below.

## Risk / Trade-off
The warm-domestic register, if pushed even slightly too far, drifts toward "cosy / greeting-card" — the exact "abuela as marketing skin" failure the brief warns against. It needs the discipline of References 9 and 10 (editorial-commerce calm, rationed color) holding it back. It is also the least cultivar-forward of the three, so Pau's hook (variety names) must be deliberately protected in the Hero type line.

# Visual Direction B — "En su Punto" (At Its Peak)

**One-line concept:** Restraint as reverence — the strawberry given silence, the way a museum gives a single object a whole wall.

**Personality:** Disciplined · luminous · quiet

**Inspired by:** Aesop · Le Fruit Studio · Farm Minerals

---

## Color Approach
- **Overall palette feeling:** luminous minimalism — cream as light itself, strawberry-red rationed to near-scarcity. The page feels lit, not decorated.
- **Dominant color role:** cream base carries everything; the brand red is used so sparingly that each appearance *means* something.
- **Accent use:** monochromatic discipline with red as the rare, deliberate accent — the fruit, one CTA per view, the variety badge. Nothing else competes.
- **Two-color discipline:** **this direction IS the two-color discipline** — it is Beat-them plan #5 expressed as a whole visual identity. The 1-neutral exists only for hairlines, dividers, and the dark bands; it never becomes a third "color".

## Typography Approach
- **Heading style:** Playfair Display (kept) — used with editorial confidence, generous leading, deliberate scale. Type is the primary craft signal.
- **Body style:** Inter (kept) — clean, geometric-leaning, quiet.
- **Weight contrast:** high but calm — clear hierarchy between heading and body, achieved through scale and space rather than heavy weights or color.

## Layout Approach
- **Grid feel:** strict, calm grid — every section aligned, nothing improvised.
- **Whitespace:** theatrical emptiness — space is the primary material. Each section gives its one element room.
- **Section rhythm:** consistent and architectural. The Products section's seven simultaneous motion events (ux-researcher finding) are pared back so each card has one quiet entrance — restraint as a fix, not just a style.

## Imagery Approach
- **Photography tone:** sparse, natural-light, textural — a single object, beautifully but quietly lit.
- **Composition:** product-focused close-ups; one subject per frame, surrounded by cream.
- **Treatment:** natural, true-to-life. No grain, no duotone — the discipline is in the *framing* and the *space*, not in a filter.
- **Sourcing (Hard Rule B):** archival family photography → next-gen family hands → elegant single-product still life. Never stock, never AI. This direction degrades most gracefully of the three if no photograph is available — because it was never depending on one.

### Hero — Path P (photograph-led)
The Hero 380×380 slot holds **one object, given silence**: a single Mágnum strawberry on warm cream (Post Familiar Wine model), or — if archival photography exists and the family consents — Mariana's hands holding that single strawberry, framed just as minimally. Either way the composition is identical: one subject, deep margins of cream, natural light. The H1 quote and the variety type line sit in disciplined whitespace. The photograph is one quiet element among several, not a stage.

### Hero — Path T (typography-only, no photograph)
Because this direction's whole identity is restraint, **Path T is not a fallback here — it is the purest expression of the direction.** The 380×380 slot becomes a typographic statement: "Mágnum · Dream · 1525" set large in Playfair Display, with the traceability chip beneath, surrounded by theatrical cream whitespace (the Le Fruit Studio model). The Hero loses nothing — the silence that would have framed a photograph now frames the type. This is the direction where the family declining photography produces *the best possible Hero*, not a compromised one.

## Motion Approach
- **Animation character:** minimal and refined — quiet fades, slow ease-outs. Motion is felt at the edge of perception, never watched.
- **Scroll behavior:** gentle IntersectionObserver fade/translate reveals only. No parallax, no pinning, no scroll-jacking. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout.

## What This Direction Communicates
A visitor feels they are in the presence of something carefully made by people with nothing to prove. The brand reads as confident, premium, and serious — the restraint itself is the trust signal. The tribute is carried by dignity and quiet rather than by warmth or story. It is the most *premium* and the most *Awwwards-aware* of the three.

## Best-fit audience
**Marc & Helena (tribute-aware)** respond most strongly — for this persona, ux-researcher noted explicitly that "the visual restraint *is* the trust signal; gloss kills the sale." **Pau (Catalan foodie)** is well served — the disciplined cultivar-forward typography respects his cultivar literacy. **Ana** appreciates the Joselito-tier restraint. **Núria** is served but gets less emotional warmth at first glance.

## Risk / Trade-off
Restraint taken to a museum register can read as *cold* — for a strawberry brand and a family tribute, a visitor could find it beautiful but distant on first scroll. The warmth has to be recovered in the AboutUs copy and the photography's natural light; if those under-deliver, the site risks feeling like an art object rather than a home.

# Visual Direction C — "Del Campo, con Calma" (From the Field, Calmly)

**One-line concept:** A quiet descent from the field to the table — the brand's story told gently through the scroll.

**Personality:** Grounded · narrative · gentle

**Inspired by:** Swiss Organic Farm · Ruinart · Canlis

---

## Color Approach
- **Overall palette feeling:** warm and grounded — cream base that subtly *warms* as the visitor descends (lightest at the Hero, a touch deeper toward AboutUs), the way light changes through a day.
- **Dominant color role:** cream base throughout; strawberry-red as the recurring thread that ties the chapters together.
- **Accent use:** intentional and rhythmic — red marks the start of each "chapter" (Hero, Products, CTA) so the scroll has punctuation.
- **Two-color discipline:** **lives inside it, with one note.** The cream "warming" through the scroll is achieved with *opacity and tone of the existing cream and neutral* — not by introducing a new color. It stays a two-color system; the 1-neutral does the tonal shift work. (Flagged for color-psychologist: confirm the warm-shift stays within tonal range of `#fdf6f5` and does not read as a second hue.)

## Typography Approach
- **Heading style:** Playfair Display (kept) — editorial, used as chapter headings down the page.
- **Body style:** Inter (kept) — humanist-leaning, warm, comfortable for the longer AboutUs narrative this direction leans on.
- **Weight contrast:** moderate — headings open each chapter clearly; body invites reading.

## Layout Approach
- **Grid feel:** single-column editorial with a clear chapter rhythm — Hero → AboutUs → Values read as a connected sequence (the "del campo a tu mesa" spine).
- **Whitespace:** generous, with deliberate pacing — space between chapters signals "take a breath".
- **Section rhythm:** a gentle narrative arc rather than uniform blocks. AboutUs is given the most room — it is the emotional centre of the descent.

## Imagery Approach
- **Photography tone:** warm, natural, slightly documentary — the honest texture of a real finca and real hands.
- **Composition:** a mix — hands and fruit at intimate scale for the tribute beats, the fruit itself at product scale for commerce beats. No wide panoramic landscapes (that would pull toward Swiss Organic Farm's scale, which is wrong here).
- **Treatment:** natural with gentle archival warmth where family photography is used.
- **Sourcing (Hard Rule B):** archival family photography → next-gen family hands → elegant non-photographic treatment. Never stock, never AI.

### Hero — Path P (photograph-led)
The Hero opens the "descent": the 380×380 slot holds a warm field-or-hands photograph — Mariana's hands among the plants (archival), or next-gen family hands at the same intimate scale. The H1 quote sits above; the traceability chip and variety line below establish *place* and *cultivar*, setting up the chapters to come. The photograph reads as the first frame of a story the scroll will continue.

### Hero — Path T (typography-only, no photograph)
The **same** narrative Hero, with the photograph replaced by a typographic chapter-opener: "Mágnum · Dream · 1525" and the place/season chip set as the first "chapter title" of the descent, in Playfair Display with generous space. The narrative spine does not depend on the photograph — the *scroll itself* carries the "del campo a tu mesa" story (Swiss Organic Farm model). The chapters that follow (AboutUs, Values) do the storytelling work; the Hero only needs to announce the journey. Path T here loses a warm image but keeps the entire narrative structure intact.

## Motion Approach
- **Animation character:** gentle and cinematic-slow — entrances tied to the scroll position so each chapter "arrives" calmly.
- **Scroll behavior:** scroll-as-narrative-spine via IntersectionObserver — sections reveal as the visitor descends. **Explicitly NOT scroll-jacking, NOT pinned** (the locked La Nonna director resolution: accessibility + performance over pinned spectacle; single rAF handler). `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses the arc to simple static sections with no loss of content.

## What This Direction Communicates
A visitor feels they are being walked, gently, from the field to their table — and through that walk, they meet Mariana. The brand reads as honest, rooted, and story-led; the tribute is woven through the journey rather than concentrated in one section. It is the most *narrative* and the most *emotionally guided* of the three.

## Best-fit audience
**Marc & Helena (tribute-aware)** respond strongly to the story-led descent. **Núria** finds the journey makes the gift story-able (her explicit decision trigger). **Laura** and **Ana** are carried along comfortably. Mild risk for **Pau** — see below.

## Risk / Trade-off
A narrative-spine direction asks the visitor to *scroll to get the value* — the cultivar-led Pau and the time-pressed mobile shopper may want the product faster than the story delivers it. The descent must keep Products reachable early and must never feel like it is withholding the buy. It is also the most motion-dependent of the three, so the reduced-motion fallback must be genuinely complete, not a degraded experience.

## Why We Did This First

Before refining colors or sections, we studied the best websites in artisanal food and in heritage/family brands — ten award-winning references in total. From them we found **three possible visual directions** for the site. Each is distinct. Each honours Mariana, and each still sells strawberries.

Two things to know before choosing:

1. **There are no wrong answers.** The direction chosen will guide every visual decision in Phase 4.
2. **None of these directions depends on a photograph of Mariana.** Each one is designed to work both *with* family photography and *without any photograph at all* — see "The Photography Question" below. The family conversation about photographs can happen calmly; it will not reset the design.

---

## Direction A — "La Mesa de la Yaya" (The Grandmother's Table)
*Warm · intimate · unhurried.* The strawberry treated the way she would have placed it on the kitchen table. The site feels like being welcomed into a home.

**References that inspired it:**
- **Post Familiar Wine** — a family brand handled with restraint, not sentimentality; product given room to breathe.
- **Buly 1803** — e-commerce that still feels like a tribute to craft; the cart never breaks the calm.
- **Beerenberg Farm** — a warm, credible family berry farm.

*(Full specification in Phase 3, Direction A above.)*

---

## Direction B — "En su Punto" (At Its Peak)
*Disciplined · luminous · quiet.* The strawberry given silence, the way a museum gives one object a whole wall. The restraint itself is the trust signal — the most premium and most Awwwards-aware of the three.

**References that inspired it:**
- **Aesop** — proof that restraint outsells noise at the premium tier; color rationed so it means something.
- **Le Fruit Studio** — a hero carried entirely by typography and space.
- **Farm Minerals** — a two-color palette held with total discipline.

*(Full specification in Phase 3, Direction B above.)*

---

## Direction C — "Del Campo, con Calma" (From the Field, Calmly)
*Grounded · narrative · gentle.* A quiet descent from the field to the table — the brand's story, and Mariana, told gently through the scroll. The most story-led of the three.

**References that inspired it:**
- **Swiss Organic Farm** — scroll used as a "from the field to your table" narrative spine.
- **Ruinart** — heritage told as a sequence of calm chapters.
- **Canlis** — a family brand that earns trust through restraint, never nostalgia kitsch.

*(Full specification in Phase 3, Direction C above.)*

## The Photography Question — important, and not blocking

Each direction above is specified **twice**: once assuming we have family photography of Mariana, and once assuming we have **none at all**.

- If the family shares an archival photograph (her hands, her in the field) and is comfortable with it being published — wonderful, the photograph-led version of the chosen direction is ready.
- If not, the typography-only version of the **same** direction is ready, and is not a downgrade. For Direction B in particular, the no-photograph version is the *purest* expression of the idea.
- We will **never** use stock photography of someone else's hands, and we will **never** use AI-generated imagery of Mariana. Those are not on the table.

This means the family conversation can be unhurried. Choosing a direction now does not pre-empt that conversation, and that conversation will not undo the choice.

---

## Our Recommendation

We recommend **Direction B — "En su Punto"** because:

1. **It is the truest expression of the homage tone.** The brief is explicit: restraint amplifies dignity; if a direction reads like an Instagram ad, it is wrong. Direction B makes restraint the entire identity — it is the homage discipline made visible. The tribute-aware audience (Marc & Helena) trusts quiet and bounces from gloss; Direction B is built for exactly that trust.
2. **It is the safest direction against the project's biggest risk.** If the family declines all photography, Directions A and C adapt well — but Direction B's no-photograph Hero is its *best* version, not a compromise. The one decision that most threatens this project cannot damage Direction B at all.
3. **It differentiates hardest from the competitors.** Every Spanish berry competitor is either decorative-busy (Fruteva, Frutos Rojos) or corporate-flat (Crowdfarming, Naranjas del Carmen). None holds restraint. Direction B is the clearest path to the Joselito tier of *craft signal* — and it does so as a surgical refinement of the existing build, not a rebuild.
4. **It is the most disciplined fit for the two-color system.** Direction B *is* Beat-them plan #5 expressed as a whole identity — it makes the color-psychologist's cream + strawberry-red + 1-neutral palette the structure of the site, not just its decoration.

**Honest trade-off we want Nil and the family to weigh:** Direction B's risk is reading as *cool* on first scroll. If the family's strongest wish is that the site feel *warm and embracing* above all — like walking into her kitchen — **Direction A** delivers that warmth more immediately, and is our second choice. Direction C is the right call only if the family has a rich story (a specific memory, a recipe, photographs that form a sequence) they want the site to *walk through*.
